Why monitor your score?

AI selection isn't static. Your CHOIVE Score changes when:

  • You publish new case studies, reviews, or content
  • Competitors strengthen their public evidence
  • AI platforms update their selection logic
  • Your category's benchmarks shift
  • Public perception of your business changes

Monitoring lets you catch improvements worth amplifying — or drops worth investigating — before they compound.

FAQ

Can I pause monitoring?

Yes. Email hello@choive.com and we'll pause or cancel your subscription immediately.

Do I need to re-run diagnostics manually?

No. Once you set up monitoring, we handle it automatically.

What if my score drops?

We'll alert you with the drop magnitude and a link to your updated report. The report shows which platform(s) changed and what evidence shifted, so you can investigate and respond.

Can I monitor multiple businesses?

Yes. Set up a separate subscription for each business ID.
