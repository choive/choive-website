Why keep an eye on your score?

Your score does not stay the same. It goes up or down when:

  • You add new reviews, stories, or pages about your business
  • Other businesses add more proof about themselves
  • AI helpers change the way they pick
  • Other businesses like yours get better or worse
  • What people say about your business changes

Keeping an eye on your score lets you catch good news you can share — or bad news you should look into — before it grows into a big problem.
