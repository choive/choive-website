CHOIVE vs Ahrefs: which one do you actually need?

Ahrefs is a well-known SEO toolset, strongest in backlink and keyword data, with newer AI-visibility features. CHOIVE runs a focused diagnostic of whether AI assistants recommend you and what to fix. Different jobs, different moments.

The short version: choose Ahrefs when you want deep SEO data — backlinks, keywords, rankings — for an ongoing program. Choose CHOIVE when you need a one-time, plain-language answer on whether AI recommends you, backed by evidence and a prioritized fix list.

Two different products for two different moments

Ahrefs is built around a large index of backlinks, keywords, and ranking data, with site audits and, more recently, AI-visibility tracking. It rewards teams that want to dig into SEO data continuously and act on it.

CHOIVE answers a different question. It asks Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini separately whether they recommend the business or a competitor, checks public evidence, scores Clarity, Trust, Difference, and Ease, and hands back the specific improvements to make first.

CHOIVE reports the provider response recorded during a run and does not claim it matches every consumer-app session. What it does give you is a clear, evidenced picture of the selection moment and how to change it.

Choose honestly

Choose Ahrefs if…

  • You want deep backlink and keyword data to work with regularly
  • You have someone who lives in SEO tooling and acts on the data
  • You are running a continuous SEO program and want AI visibility alongside it
  • You are comfortable with a recurring subscription

Choose CHOIVE if…

  • You want to know today whether AI recommends you — or a competitor
  • You want a plain-language answer and evidence, not raw SEO data to interpret
  • You want prioritized fixes and ready-to-use assets you can ship
  • You want a focused diagnostic before investing in a full SEO data platform

Can you use both?

Yes. Use CHOIVE to diagnose the AI selection gap precisely and get your first fixes, then use a data toolset like Ahrefs for the ongoing backlink, keyword, and ranking work that supports it. The diagnostic tells you what matters most right now; the toolset supports the long game.

How CHOIVE makes the comparison

CHOIVE separates three things that are often mixed together: what each AI provider recommended during the run, which company is the closest verified purchasing substitute, and how strong the subject business’s own public evidence is. The CHOIVE score is calculated from the subject business evidence, not from whether an AI provider happened to recommend it. The complete process and limitations are published in the CHOIVE methodology, and the principles behind it in the CHOIVE AI Selection Standard.
