Honest comparison

CHOIVE vs an SEO agency: which one do you actually need?

An SEO agency is a service: human strategists doing ongoing, custom work for you. CHOIVE is a product: an instant, standardized diagnostic of whether AI assistants recommend you and what to fix. They are not rivals so much as different stages of the same job.

The short version: choose an agency when you want experts to do the ongoing work for you. Choose CHOIVE when you first want an independent, evidence-based answer on where you stand with AI — which also makes a far better brief for any agency you hire.

Two different products for two different moments

A good SEO or AI-visibility agency brings human strategists, custom execution, and accountability over months. It is the right choice when you want the work done for you and have the budget for a retainer.

CHOIVE is standardized and instant. It asks Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini separately whether they recommend you, checks public evidence, scores Clarity, Trust, Difference, and Ease, and returns prioritized fixes and ready-to-use assets — the same method for every business, including CHOIVE itself.

Because CHOIVE is independent of any agency, its diagnosis is a neutral baseline. You can hand the Report to whoever does the work and measure progress against it later.

Choose honestly

Choose an SEO agency if…

  • You want experts to do the ongoing work for you, not do it yourself
  • You have budget for a retainer and want accountability over months
  • Your needs are complex enough to warrant custom strategy and execution
  • You value a long-term human relationship over a one-time answer

Choose CHOIVE if…

  • You want an independent, evidence-based answer before you spend on a retainer
  • You want the answer in under a minute, free, with no sales call
  • You want a neutral baseline and brief to hold any agency accountable to
  • You want prioritized fixes and ready-to-use assets you can act on immediately

Can you use both?

Yes — and they complement each other well. Run CHOIVE first to get an independent baseline, a clear score, and a prioritized list of what matters. Then hire an agency to execute against it, and re-run the diagnostic later to measure progress against the same rubric. CHOIVE gives the agency a sharper brief and gives you an objective way to judge the results.

How CHOIVE makes the comparison

CHOIVE separates three things that are often mixed together: what each AI provider recommended during the run, which company is the closest verified purchasing substitute, and how strong the subject business’s own public evidence is. The CHOIVE score is calculated from the subject business evidence, not from whether an AI provider happened to recommend it. The complete process and limitations are published in the CHOIVE methodology, and the principles behind it in the CHOIVE AI Selection Standard.
