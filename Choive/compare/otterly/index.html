CHOIVE vs Otterly.AI: which one do you actually need?

Otterly.AI is an AI-search monitoring tool that tracks how brands are mentioned and cited in AI answers. CHOIVE runs a focused, point-in-time diagnostic of whether AI recommends you, with the evidence behind the score and what to fix. Monitoring and diagnosing solve different problems.

The short version: choose Otterly.AI when you want to monitor brand mentions and citations in AI search over time. Choose CHOIVE when you need a one-time, evidence-based answer on whether AI recommends you today, why, and what to improve first.

Two different products for two different moments

Otterly.AI is built for monitoring: tracking prompts, brand mentions, and citations across AI search so you can watch them over time. It suits teams that already run an AI-visibility program and want ongoing signal.

CHOIVE is built for diagnosis. It asks Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini separately whether they recommend you, inspects public evidence, scores Clarity, Trust, Difference, and Ease, and returns prioritized fixes and ready-to-use assets from a single run.

Being mentioned is not the same as being the recommendation. CHOIVE focuses on the selection moment — who AI actually recommends — reports the response recorded during that run, and states plainly where it is uncertain.

Choose honestly

Choose Otterly.AI if…

  • You want to monitor brand mentions and citations in AI search continuously
  • You already know your gaps and want ongoing signal over time
  • You have someone who will watch and act on the monitoring data
  • Continuous tracking, not a one-time answer, is your priority

Choose CHOIVE if…

  • You don’t yet know whether AI recommends you — or a competitor
  • You want to know who AI recommends, not just where you are mentioned
  • You want the answer in under a minute, free, with no account
  • You need an evidence-based baseline and fix plan before monitoring pays off

Can you use both?

Yes, in the right order. Run CHOIVE first to learn whether AI recommends you, understand the evidence, and close the foundational gaps. Once there is a program worth watching, a monitoring tool like Otterly.AI can track mentions and citations over time. Diagnose and fix first; monitor when there is something worth monitoring.

How CHOIVE makes the comparison

CHOIVE separates three things that are often mixed together: what each AI provider recommended during the run, which company is the closest verified purchasing substitute, and how strong the subject business’s own public evidence is. The CHOIVE score is calculated from the subject business evidence, not from whether an AI provider happened to recommend it. The complete process and limitations are published in the CHOIVE methodology, and the principles behind it in the CHOIVE AI Selection Standard.
